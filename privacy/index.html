Overview

Cheridose is a medication reminder and tracking aid. The app does not require a Cheridose account for everyday use, and Cheridose does not operate a server that receives your medication schedules or dose history.

We do not sell personal information. The current app and this website do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter into Cheridose is stored locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • your profile name and timezone settings;
  • medication names, strengths, and instructions;
  • Dose Events, schedules, reminders, and notification-sound choices;
  • records of doses marked taken, late, missed, skipped, snoozed, or partial;
  • travel plans and clinician-directed taper schedules.

This information is used on your device to calculate upcoming Dose Events, show history, schedule local notifications, and provide features you choose to use.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Cheridose asks iOS for notification permission and uses the device's local-notification system. Apple controls notification permissions and delivery at the operating-system level. You can change notification access in iOS Settings or within Cheridose.

Apple services and external links

App Store purchases

If you buy or restore Cheridose Premium, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. Cheridose reads the resulting purchase and entitlement information on your device so it can unlock Premium features. Apple handles payment details under its own privacy policy; Cheridose does not receive your full payment-card information.

External links

This website may link to the App Store or Apple. Visiting an external website or app allows that provider to receive the information normally sent by your browser or device, such as an IP address and device information.

Retention, deletion, and security

Cheridose data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove locally stored Cheridose data using the app's “Forget all data” control. Removing the app from your device also removes its local app data. Cheridose does not currently provide cloud backup or multi-device sync, so deleted local data may not be recoverable.

Local information is protected by the security of your device and operating system. Use a device passcode and keep iOS current. No local storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

Cheridose is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Cheridose server.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when Cheridose changes. The latest version will remain available on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

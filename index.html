Why Cheridose exists

Made for Cheryl.
Made for you.

Cheridose began on a family trip to the Azores. My mom, Cheryl, was being treated for cancer, and crossing time zones made an already complicated medication routine harder to track.

I looked for an app that could handle travel across time zones, tapering schedules, medications being added or removed, and a clear history of doses taken, late, or missed—without making everyday use feel complicated. I couldn’t find one that fit, so I decided to build it for her.

Cheridose was made for Cheryl. Now it’s available to anyone who could use the same clarity and care.

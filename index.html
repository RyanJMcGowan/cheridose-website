Why I made Cheridose

I made this for my mom. And yours, too.

Cheridose began on a family trip to the Azores. My mom, Cheryl, was being treated for cancer. Crossing time zones made an already difficult medication routine even harder to follow.

I looked for an app that could handle the details real routines involve: time-zone changes, tapering schedules, medications that come and go, and a clear history—including late and missed doses. I couldn’t find it. So I built it for her.

Her name is Cheryl. I made Cheridose for her. Now it’s here for anyone who needs it.
